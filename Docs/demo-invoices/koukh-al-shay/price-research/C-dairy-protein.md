# Dairy, Chilled and Frozen Protein - UAE Price Research (2026-09-01)

Research method: firecrawl-search + firecrawl-scrape against named foodservice distributors,
brand sites, and retail sanity ceilings.
A significant new source turned up mid-research and is used heavily below: **horecamarket.ae**,
a genuine Dubai/Abu Dhabi HORECA (Hotel/Restaurant/Catering) wholesaler, supplier of record
Mirbat Foodstuff Trading LLC.
Every listing on that site states its price as "AED X.XX Plus 5% VAT" - explicit ex-VAT wholesale
pricing, per kilogram, with a stated minimum order box weight.
That is exactly the evidence class this task asked for, so it is treated as a priority source
alongside Tradeling.

All AED-per-kg / AED-per-litre figures below show the arithmetic.
Ex-VAT conversions from a stated VAT-inclusive price are shown as `price ÷ 1.05`.

## What surprised me

1. **The chicken price was not a one-off typo - it's a pattern.** Chicken wings at AED 2.90/kg is just as far off reality (real: AED 11.50-18.40/kg) as boneless breast was. Condensed milk (AED 7.65 vs ~19.86/kg) and evaporated milk (AED 4.69 vs ~13.72/L) are also roughly 2.5-3x understated. Whatever produced the AED 3.45/kg chicken number seems to have systematically lowballed the higher-value processed/imported items while getting the simpler fresh-adjacent items (yoghurt, paneer, ghee, butter) nearly exactly right. That's a useful diagnostic on its own: it looks like whoever built the demo seed either copied older prices, or specifically discounted branded/processed SKUs, rather than making random errors.

2. **A single supplier (horecamarket.ae) turned out to be the best evidence source in the whole slice, and it wasn't on the priority list.** It states every price as "AED X.XX Plus 5% VAT" - genuinely ex-VAT, per kilogram, with a stated minimum-order box weight - for a real Dubai/Abu Dhabi HORECA wholesaler (Mirbat Foodstuff Trading LLC). That is a materially higher evidence standard than most named-brand retail listings, which routinely don't state their VAT basis at all. Worth keeping this domain in mind for future UAE foodservice price research.

3. **Brand tier swings the "right" number by 2-4x on several lines**, not just chicken. Cooking cream ranges from AED 5.60/L (private label) to AED 19.08/L (Almarai) to AED 23.00/L (specialty sour cream) depending purely on brand choice - all legitimate wholesale prices, just different products. Same story with butter (blend margarine at AED 12/kg vs pure butter at AED 26.48/kg) and milk powder (filled catering powder at AED 6.12/kg vs genuine Nido at AED 36+/kg). For a costing model this matters more than getting "the" market price exactly right - the real question is which tier the actual invoices show.

4. **"Curry cut" and "PUD" are spec terms that don't reliably exist as searchable SKUs.** Every UAE supplier lists beef by named cut (brisket, striploin, topside, tongue) and prawns by count-per-kg grade (40/60, 26/30, 11/15), not by the generic catering shorthand used in the source table. Getting these two lines right needs either a real invoice to match against, or a decision from the founder on exactly which cut/grade the menu recipes assume.

### Boneless chicken - what a Dubai cafeteria really pays

The current AED 3.45/kg is not defensible under any interpretation I could find. Four independent sources - Falcon Fresh (AED 18.70/kg), Martoo/Sadia (AED 21.83/kg), and two separate explicit ex-VAT wholesale listings from horecamarket.ae, a genuine Dubai/Abu Dhabi HORECA supplier (AED 16.00/kg and AED 20.00/kg) - converge tightly in the **AED 16-22/kg** range for frozen boneless skinless chicken breast, Brazilian origin (Sadia/Aurora-class brands, which is exactly the Sadia/Seara/BRF import channel the task brief pointed at). The horecamarket.ae numbers are the strongest evidence in the entire slice: stated per kilogram, stated ex-VAT ("Plus 5% VAT"), with a stated minimum order (12kg box), from a supplier whose whole business is selling this exact product to hotels, restaurants and cafeterias. A realistic demo number is **AED 16-18/kg ex-VAT for a 10-12kg carton of Brazilian frozen boneless skinless breast**, i.e. roughly **five to six times the current AED 3.45/kg**. Given that this is the item the closing money moment of the demo rides on, I would not ship anything under AED 15/kg without a specific reason to believe the cafeteria is buying an unusually cheap tier (e.g. bone-in leg meat sold as "boneless" after deboning in-house, which does happen but should be a documented decision, not a silent default).
