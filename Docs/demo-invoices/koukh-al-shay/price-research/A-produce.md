# Fresh Produce — UAE Price Research (2026-09-01)

Evidence collected 2026-09-01 via web search and page scrapes.
All Al Aweer figures come from alaweerprices.com, a third-party site that says it compiles indicative wholesale trader quotes from Al Aweer Central Market each morning and normalises them to AED/kg.
It is not the Dubai Municipality itself, but it is the closest thing found to an Al Aweer bulletin, and its data is dated 2026-08-30 (one day before this research), refreshed daily.
It does not state whether its quotes are ex-VAT or inclusive — flagged "not stated" throughout, per the rules of evidence.

## What surprised me

- **Spring onion is the biggest miss in the "too cheap" direction**: current AED 8.00/kg vs two independent sources (a HORECA bulk supplier and Carrefour retail) both landing almost exactly on AED 15/kg — the current number is roughly half of reality.
- **Cauliflower is also badly underpriced**: current AED 3.00/kg vs AED 5.20/kg wholesale (Al Aweer) and AED 6.62/kg ex-VAT retail (Lulu) — both sources agree the current figure understates cost by 70–120%.
- **Coconut grated is understated by ~60%**: current AED 14.00/kg vs AED 22.38/kg ex-VAT at Sidco Foods (though this rests on one source).
- **Mushroom looks understated too, by roughly 50–65%** (current 13.50 vs 20–22.50), but the evidence for mushroom is the weakest in the set — even the "bulk" box price sits inside the retail range rather than clearly below it, which is a red flag worth checking with an actual invoice rather than trusting this research alone.
- **Capsicum green is the most confusing line**: the one wholesale (Al Aweer) quote available says current AED 4.20/kg is more than double the real wholesale price (1.71/kg), but ordinary retail runs 8.8–10.5/kg ex-VAT — five to six times the wholesale figure, a spread wide enough that I would not trust either number without a real supplier quote.
- **Onion, tomato, ginger, lemon, garlic peeled (per the Sidco figure), and ginger garlic paste all turned out close to the current placeholder numbers** — reassuring, since it suggests whoever set the original placeholders was roughly right for the "everyday" items and mainly wrong on the items that are less commonly priced (leafy greens, mushroom, coconut, spring onion).
- **Curry leaves and coriander pack sizes look unrealistic**: nobody in the UAE market seems to sell curry leaves in 500g bags or, as commonly, coriander doesn't move in exactly matching bulk units either — both are almost always sold in small retail packs (50–250g) even by "wholesale-flavoured" sites, so the per-kg price is inherently inflated by small-pack economics. I'd recommend re-shaping those two catalog entries to smaller packs rather than trusting a straight-line per-kg extrapolation.
