## What surprised me

- **Ketchup and sunflower oil check out almost exactly** against real bulk-format UAE listings (Vital Foods 5 kg jerry can ketchup at 5.45/l ex-VAT vs invoice 5.20/l; Nawar 5 l tin oil at 7.50/l ex-VAT vs invoice 7.80/l). These two lines look trustworthy as-is.
- **The takeaway container (#14) is the biggest red flag.** Every wholesale 750 ml tub+lid combo I found from a real UAE B2B disposables distributor (Falcon Pack) priced at roughly 0.58/pc - not far off double the invoice's 0.33/pc. Even after applying a wholesale discount off the retail price, the wholesale figure I found is still well above the invoice.
- **Vinegar and red chilli sauce likely undercount too, but on weaker evidence.** Vinegar's best checkout-verified figure (Tradeling gallon carton) implies ~4.18/l ex-VAT vs the invoice's 2.80/l - a 5 l can at that rate would run ~21 AED, not 14. Chilli sauce has no bulk-can SKU findable at all; every small-bottle-carton price I found implies 11.67-22/l vs the invoice's 5.80/l. I'd treat these two as "probably underpriced on the invoice" rather than "confirmed wrong," since neither has a clean like-for-like bulk SKU match.
- **Two disposable-packaging lines (cake box + fork, and both flask sizes) simply don't exist as indexed, price-tagged UAE SKUs** in the combo form the invoice describes. The cake box + fork combo and the 2 l disposable flask especially - I'd flag these to the founder as "we don't actually know the market price of this exact item" rather than quietly trusting the current number.
- **Delivery/paper cups are a mixed bag**: the medium and large delivery cups, and the large paper cup, land close to the invoice once you price cup and lid separately from a real distributor (Falcon Pack) and add them together. But the small delivery cup and small paper cup lines look off, mostly because I could not find an exact-size lid SKU to match the small cup sizes - the invoice may be buying (or should buy) the plain "Paper Cup" line rather than the insulated "Ripple Cup" line for the cheaper sizes, since that's where the real savings show up.
