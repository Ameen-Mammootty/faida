# Dry Goods and Spices — UAE Wholesale Price Research

Date seen for all rows: 2026-09-01.
Primary source: Tradeling (tradeling.com), a B2B marketplace — every listing used states its own VAT basis on the product page (either "Incl. VAT" shown directly, or "Excl. Import Charges" which is noted as such, never assumed).
Where a listing did not clearly state VAT basis, that is called out explicitly.
UAE VAT = 5%, so ex-VAT = incl-VAT price ÷ 1.05.

Al Douri's own site (aldourigroup.com) returned a connection reset on every attempt — could not get a page to load, so it contributes nothing here.
Chef Middle East's product pages hide price behind a login wall — usable for confirming SKUs exist, not for prices.
MH Enterprises returned no indexed results for spice search terms.
Bayara.ae (brand site) does not sell direct/does not show consumer prices — Bayara prices below all come from Bayara-branded listings sold through Tradeling.
So in practice this whole sheet is Tradeling-sourced (in line with priority order — it's the named distributor that actually had searchable, priced, in-stock catering packs), cross-checked against Lulu retail for saffron only.

- **Cinnamon stick, turmeric, and Kashmiri chilli powder are all understated by 35–75%** in the current sheet, consistent with a pattern of the small-pack Indian/Pakistani masala items being underpriced across the board while the "exotic" items (saffron, cardamom) got closer-to-real numbers.
