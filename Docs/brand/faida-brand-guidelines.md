# Faida brand direction

## Executive direction

Faida should feel like the calm, capable operating layer behind a busy cafeteria.
It should be warm enough to belong in hospitality, precise enough to handle financial evidence, and restrained enough to earn trust from a multi-branch owner.

The identity must never depend on an AI story.
The product promise is evidence, clarity, and control.

## Product truth from the PRD

### Core problem and moment

A cafeteria owner already has invoices, supplier messages, sales reports, and branch activity, but cannot quickly see which ingredients, items, suppliers, or branches are helping or harming profit.

The defining product moment is simple.
A branch forwards a supplier invoice on WhatsApp, Faida reads and checks it, the user confirms it with one reply, and a price movement becomes visible before it quietly erodes margin.

### Target audience

The primary audience is a GCC cafeteria owner or head-office operator managing one outlet or a multi-branch karak and paratha chain.

The secondary audience is the branch manager or salesman who forwards invoices and confirms the occasional field without learning a new workflow.

Consultants and brand-level operators form a third audience for onboarding, recipe coverage, and portfolio views.

### Desired perception

- Modern hospitality, not generic fintech.
- Highly professional, not corporate-cold.
- Clean and direct, not sparse for fashion's sake.
- Trusted and evidence-led, not magical or predictive.
- Independent and regionally relevant, not an imitation of Swiggy, Zomato, or WhatsApp.

## Positioning language

### Recommended one-line promise

> Faida turns the invoices and sales you already have into a clear, trusted view of what is helping or hurting profit.

### Recommended brand line

> Profit, in plain sight.

The promise explains the mechanism and outcome.
The brand line compresses the position into a memorable phrase without overclaiming net-profit accuracy.

### Useful supporting lines

- See what changed before margin slips.
- Every branch, one clear picture.
- From invoice photo to trusted answer.
- Forward it. Check it. Know the impact.

Avoid claims such as "AI-powered profit," "autonomous finance," or "real-time net profit."
The PRD is explicit that AI only reads documents and that incomplete data must stay visibly incomplete.

## Logo concepts

### 01. The Margin Fold - recommended

Three ascending folded tiles form a compact motion from left to right.
The fold connects to invoices and handover, the three-part rhythm suggests multiple branches, and the rise signals improving visibility rather than a literal growth promise.

This route is distinctive at small sizes and can become a strong app icon, WhatsApp avatar, browser favicon, and navigation mark.

### 02. The Branch Table

Three rounded tiles and one rising gold quarter represent a branch network producing a clearer result.
It feels highly digital and modular, but the basic tile language is more common in SaaS branding.

### 03. The Proof Mark

A receipt and a confirmed result sit inside a circular seal.
It communicates evidence and trust clearly, but it is more detailed and feels closer to compliance software.

### 04. The Ledger F

A geometric letter F is built from a vertical ledger and two invoice lines.
It is direct and useful, but colder and less ownable than the recommended route.

### 05. The Karak Halo

A warm circular form contains two rising elements.
It carries the strongest cafeteria association, but it may limit the brand if Faida expands beyond small-format hospitality.

## Recommended logo system

Use the full-color horizontal lockup by default.
Use the stacked lockup only when the available space is centered or nearly square.
Use the mark alone only after the Faida name has already appeared in the experience or when the context is unambiguous, such as an app icon.
Use the one-color version for stamps, receipts, embroidery, laser marking, and monochrome print.

### Clear space

Keep clear space equal to the width of the gold center fold on every side of the mark or lockup.
No text, border, icon, or crop should enter this area.

### Minimum size

- Mark only: 24 px digital or 7 mm print.
- Horizontal lockup: 120 px digital or 28 mm print.
- Below 120 px, use the mark plus a standard text label rather than forcing the full wordmark.

### Do not

- Do not add gradients, glow, glass effects, or drop shadows.
- Do not recolor individual panels outside the approved palette.
- Do not rotate, stretch, outline, or place the mark inside another arbitrary shape.
- Do not pair it with a currency symbol, chart arrow, sparkles, or AI imagery.
- Do not place the full-color logo over busy photography.

## Color system

### Core palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Date Palm | `#153E35` | Primary brand field, buttons, navigation, large type |
| Karak Gold | `#E3A13B` | Warm accent, selected moments, mark center fold |
| Warm Cream | `#FBF6EC` | Branded background and hospitality warmth |
| Service Ink | `#172421` | Headlines, body text, monochrome logo |

### Supporting palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Sage | `#9CC8B3` | Secondary data fills and calm highlights |
| Mist | `#E7EFEA` | Panels, dividers, quiet status backgrounds |
| Slate | `#52625D` | Secondary text and labels |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Data surfaces and document review panels |

### Functional palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Confirmed Green | `#1D6D50` | Verified or positive status with icon and label |
| Attention Amber | `#80520A` | Review-required text on pale gold surfaces |
| Critical Plum | `#65415F` | Error and blocked states without using red |

Color must never carry meaning alone.
Every status also needs an icon and a plain-language label such as "Verified," "Needs review," or "Blocked."

Karak Gold is an accent, not a body-text color on cream or white.
Use Date Palm or Service Ink for text in those contexts.

### Recommended ratio

Use roughly 60 percent cream or white, 25 percent Date Palm, 10 percent neutral supporting color, and no more than 5 percent Karak Gold on a typical screen or presentation page.
This keeps the identity warm without making the interface feel promotional.

## Typography

### Brand and marketing

Use **Manrope** for brand headlines, campaign copy, and the working wordmark.
Use weight 600 for headlines and 700 only for short labels or emphasis.
Set large headlines with slightly tight tracking between `-0.02em` and `-0.03em`.

### Product interface

Use **Inter** for navigation, body copy, forms, tables, and dense product UI.
Use 400 for body text, 500 for controls, and 600 for section headings and key values.
Enable tabular numerals for money, quantities, dates, and comparisons.

### Arabic

Use **Noto Sans Arabic** for Arabic UI and documents.
Match perceived size and weight with the Latin text rather than copying the same point size mechanically.
Keep English numerals in financial tables unless product research establishes a different customer preference.

### Type behavior

- Use sentence case for headings, actions, and status labels.
- Avoid all caps except for small metadata labels with generous tracking.
- Keep headline numbers rounded in dashboards and exact amounts in invoice detail, following the PRD.
- Never label purchases divided by net sales as "food cost %."
- Never label branch contribution as net profit.

## Shape and layout language

Use simple geometric forms with a modest corner radius.
The brand should feel composed, not bubbly.

Favor structured grids, wide breathing room, clear divider lines, and one dominant action per surface.
Use cream selectively around brand moments and white for dense evidence surfaces such as invoice review.

Avoid floating gradient orbs, glass cards, excessive pills, noisy icon collections, faux-3D illustrations, and dashboard decoration without informational purpose.

## Image direction

When photography is needed, show real GCC cafeteria environments with natural working detail.
Use invoices, counters, tea preparation, supplier crates, and hands at work as evidence of operations, not as styled food glamour.

Prefer warm natural light, real material texture, and candid framing.
Avoid oversaturated food photography, generic smiling-office teams, robots, holograms, and synthetic data overlays.

## Voice

Faida speaks like an experienced operator who has checked the numbers.
The voice is brief, specific, calm, and helpful.

Say "Milk powder is up AED 4.00 since the last confirmed invoice."
Do not say "Our intelligent engine detected a significant price anomaly."

Say "This total needs review."
Do not say "Low confidence output generated."

Say "We could not read this photo. Try a straighter one, or enter the total."
Do not blame the user or expose model terminology.

## Final recommendation

Proceed with The Margin Fold, the Date Palm and Karak Gold palette, Manrope for brand expression, Inter for product UI, and the line "Profit, in plain sight."

Before trademark filing or a public launch, run a professional similarity search and refine the wordmark into custom outlined letterforms.
