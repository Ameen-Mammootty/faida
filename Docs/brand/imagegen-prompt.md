# Image-generation exploration record

The built-in image-generation tool was used for early shape exploration only.
The final logo sources in this handover are manually constructed SVGs because deterministic vector geometry is the appropriate production format for a logo.

## Mode

Built-in image generation.

## Initial prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: visual logo exploration board for a professional SaaS brand
Primary request: Create four original, clean geometric logo directions for the brand name "faida", a profit-visibility platform for GCC cafeterias and multi-branch restaurant operators. The ideas should subtly combine hospitality, invoice clarity, upward business progress, and branch operations without using literal currency symbols.
Style/medium: flat vector identity exploration, highly refined, modern restaurant-tech feel, enterprise-grade, simple and scalable, balanced negative space
Composition/framing: 2 by 2 grid on a warm cream background, generous spacing, each direction shown as one symbol beside a lowercase wordmark. Render the word "faida" exactly and only once per direction. No captions, no concept numbers, no extra text.
Color palette: deep date-palm green #153E35, karak gold #E3A13B, warm cream #FBF6EC, near-black ink #172421. Use at most two colors per mark.
Typography: geometric humanist sans serif, confident and friendly, precise spacing, not playful
Constraints: original designs only; strong silhouette; balanced geometry; flat colors; no red; no gradients; no 3D; no mockup scene; no shadows; no watermark; do not imitate Swiggy, Zomato, WhatsApp, or any existing logo
Avoid: AI sparkles, magic wands, brains, neural nodes, chat bubbles, robot imagery, obvious bar charts, coins, dollar signs, utensils, chef hats, mascots, swooshes, stock-logo look
```

## Corrective edit prompt

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit
Asset type: corrected flat logo exploration board
Primary request: Change only the presentation styling of the supplied logo exploration board. Keep the four logo concepts, their 2 by 2 positions, and the exact lowercase word "faida" beside each concept. Replace the black/dark background with one perfectly flat warm cream background #FBF6EC. Remove every glow, shadow, halo, transparency effect, gradient, vignette, lighting effect, and texture. Recolor all dark green parts to solid #153E35, all gold parts to solid #E3A13B, and all text to solid #172421 for strong readable contrast.
Style/medium: crisp flat vector identity sheet
Constraints: preserve the four concepts and composition; flat colors only; exact word "faida" four times; no extra text; no red; no gradients; no glow; no shadows; no 3D; no watermark
```
