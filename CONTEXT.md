# Faida

Profit visibility for GCC cafeterias and multi-branch chains, fed through WhatsApp: supplier invoices in, costed menus and branch figures out.
This glossary holds the words the product uses and the words it refuses, so a screen, a message and a conversation say the same thing.

## Language

### Money and margin

**Contribution**:
What a dish or a branch keeps after the ingredients it sold, at the prices in force, said as an estimate with the share of sales it covers beside it.
_Avoid_: Profit, net profit, margin (for a branch), profit share

**Purchases ÷ net sales**:
Confirmed supplier purchases over ex-VAT till sales for a branch over a window, cash basis.
_Avoid_: Food cost %

### Staff incentive

**Scheme month**:
The calendar month over which a branch's incentive is scored and paid; its targets are frozen when it starts.
_Avoid_: Period, campaign, cycle

**Push week**:
A Monday-to-Sunday week inside a scheme month that carries its own push list; a week under way is frozen, a week not yet started may be re-aimed.
_Avoid_: Sprint, promo week

**Push list**:
The push items of one push week, grouped on the screen by the menu's own category, one to three per category as guidance.
_Avoid_: Focus list, specials, promotions

**Push item**:
A menu item the owner has chosen for a push week, with a portion target for that week; it must already map to a till name.
_Avoid_: Hero item, featured item, upsell

**Target**:
An absolute figure the owner types: portions of a push item for a push week, or net sales of a branch for a scheme month.
_Avoid_: Goal, quota, baseline

**Pool**:
The amount a branch's team has earned for a scheme month, before it is split by role.
_Avoid_: Commission, bonus fund, kitty

**Role share**:
The percentages of a pool that go to the manager, the supervisor and the sales team, set per tenant and editable at any time; a change applies from the next scheme month, and a running month keeps the shares it started with.
_Avoid_: Split, cut

**Bonus**:
The staff-facing word for what the pool pays out.
_Avoid_: Profit share, commission

**Statement**:
A scheme month's scores and pool as read from loaded sales days; provisional until every day of the month is loaded, final once the owner approves it.
_Avoid_: Report, payslip, settlement

**Scoreboard**:
The daily WhatsApp card to a branch's registered phone carrying the current push list, the portions and net sales so far against their targets, and the newest loaded day.
_Avoid_: Leaderboard, dashboard (for the branch message)
